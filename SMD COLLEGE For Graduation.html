Admission Process

Your journey to quality higher education starts here

How to Apply

1

Application Form

Fill out the online application form with personal and academic details. Download from our website or collect from campus.

2

Submit Documents

Submit required documents including 12th mark sheet, transfer certificate, and other supporting documents.

3

Merit Assessment

Applications will be evaluated based on academic performance and merit criteria for the chosen program.

4

Admission Confirmation

Selected candidates will receive admission letter. Confirm admission by paying fees within the stipulated time.

Admission Information

Eligibility

12th pass from recognized board with minimum 50% marks

Application Fee

₹500 (Refundable)

Admission Period

June - August

Seat Capacity

200 seats per program

Required Documents:

  • 12th Mark Sheet & Certificate
  • 10th Mark Sheet
  • AdharCard
  • Transfer Certificate
  • Character Certificate
  • 2 Passport Size Photographs
  • Caste Certificate (if applicable)

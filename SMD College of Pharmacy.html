Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharma)

A D.Pharm (Diploma in Pharmacy) is a 2-year undergraduate diploma program that provides foundational knowledge and practical skills in pharmaceutical sciences.

The course covers subjects like pharmacology, pharmacognosy, pharmaceutical chemistry, and hospital and clinical pharmacy. It prepares students for roles in community pharmacies, hospitals, and other healthcare settings.

After completing the course, graduates are eligible to register as pharmacists and work in various pharmaceutical sectors or pursue higher education like B.Pharm (Bachelor of Pharmacy).

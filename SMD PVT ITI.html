About SMD Private ITI

Empowering youth with practical skills for a successful career in industry

What is ITI?

Industrial Training Institute (ITI) provides vocational training in various technical and non-technical trades. Our programs are designed to equip students with practical skills that are directly applicable in the industry.

We focus on skill-based education that bridges the gap between theoretical knowledge and real-world application, ensuring our graduates are job-ready from day one.

Industry Partnership Approach

We collaborate with leading industries to update our curriculum, provide internships, and facilitate placements for our students.
